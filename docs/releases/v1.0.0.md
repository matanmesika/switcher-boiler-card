## Switcher Boiler Card v1.0.0

First release. A Home Assistant card that looks and works like the Switcher app for a Switcher water heater.

- **Home:** turn the dial to set a timer. Each full turn adds an hour, completed turns stay visible with a +1h / +2h badge, and the arrow follows the turning direction. Power button, Auto Shutdown, water temperature, current and power in one row.
- **Schedule:** add, edit, enable and delete weekly schedules (Scheduler integration).
- **History:** every run of the last 30 days with its length, plus electricity costs for any date range and a month-by-month comparison.
- **Automatic setup:** pick the boiler switch and the card finds the Switcher's own sensors. Runs and schedules use the Switcher's built-in timer.
- **Manual setup:** a visual editor for every sensor, timer and script, or YAML.
